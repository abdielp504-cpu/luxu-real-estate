# Track 09: Optimization & SEO Excellence

## Goal
Transform the Luxe Real Estate platform into a high-performance, search-engine-optimized masterpiece. This involves implementing advanced SEO strategies, ensuring lightning-fast load times, and providing a flawless sharing experience across social media.

## Features
- **Dynamic Metadata:** Automated title, description, and keyword generation for every property and page.
- **OpenGraph & Social:** Sophisticated social sharing previews (OpenGraph/Twitter) with property images.
- **Structured Data:** Implementation of JSON-LD (Schema.org) for Property listings to enhance search results (rich snippets).
- **Search Engine Discovery:** Automated `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` generation.
- **Image Optimization:** Rigorous audit of all `next/image` usage, including priority loading and responsive sizes.
- **Performance Caching:** Fine-tuning Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR) and Supabase data caching.

## Design Standards
- **Performance First:** Aim for "Green" Lighthouse scores across all metrics.
- **Accessibility (a11y):** Ensure high contrast and screen reader compatibility without compromising the luxury aesthetic.
- **Semantic HTML:** Use proper tags to ensure both search engines and assistive technologies understand the content hierarchy.
